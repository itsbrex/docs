REQUEST BOT

Turn a rough request into a brief, assign it to specialists, and review their work in one conversation.

OverviewSupportPrivacy policyTerms of service

How it works

Ask for a short Slack or email request and a longer delegation brief. For a larger job, ask the team to divide the work. Specialists can produce comparisons, checklists, decisions and drafts, then hand them to the next role.

Try it

“Procurement and Finance, compare two vendor quotes. Alder charges $900 a month. Birch charges $1,000 a month. Include a one-year cost table and flag the missing implementation fees.”

Availability

REQUEST BOT is free and offers no purchases. Brian Roach publishes the plugin. The interactive Team Desk is available in compatible MCP Apps hosts.
